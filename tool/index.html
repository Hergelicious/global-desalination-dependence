Index = 100 × [(water stress ÷ 5) + (fossil electricity share ÷ 100)] ÷ 2. It is a descriptive screening measure of where scarcity and fossil electricity coincide, not a measure of lock-in itself and not a prediction of desalination demand. Data: WRI Aqueduct 4.0 (2023), World Development Indicators (latest since 2010), AQUASTAT via Gapminder. Companion to "Water scarcity and fossil electricity shape global desalination dependence" (Hassan, 2026); see the paper for definitions and limitations.
